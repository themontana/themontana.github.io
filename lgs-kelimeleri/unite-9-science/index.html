Paragraf sorusu
Marie Curie was a famous scientist. She was born in Poland in 1867. She discovered radium and polonium. She won two Nobel Prizes in Physics and Chemistry.

Which of the following is NOT true about Marie Curie?

  1. She discovered radium and polonium.
  2. She was a famous scientist.
  3. She won three Nobel Prizes.
  4. She was born in Poland.
Cevabı ve açıklamayı göster

Doğru cevap: C) She won three Nobel Prizes.

Metinde iki Nobel ödülü yazıyor. Üç ödül kazandığını söyleyen seçenek metne aykırı; soru NOT true diyor.
